# ShopSphere — Business Requirements

## 1. Business Background

ShopShpere is a B2B2C ecommerce platform connecting sellers and consumers. Sellers list products on ShopSphere and consumers can buy products from ShopShpere. ShopShpere caters wide variety of products including daily consumer goods to industrial equipments fulfilling each type of consumer demands. It also supports multiple sellers from different categories and segments. To maintain the quality of products and services, ShopSphere has to ensure that sellers are providing genuine products and the consumers will get their orders delivered on time.

## 2. Business Problem

The major business problem management might face is related to revenue, customer retention, seller performance, order delays, customer support and various other operational aspects. To overcome these business problems ShopSphere is planning to implement an ecommerce analytics platform to provide real time insights to management.

## 3. Project Objective

The project objectives are to provide insights on how the revenue is changing with time, who are the potential and genuine customers, in which region the most orders are places, which regions failed to deliver the orders on time, which sellers are performing well and which sellers need improvement.

## 4. Stakeholders

### 4.1 CEO

The decision CEO is to make is that how to increase the overall revenue of the company and improve profits.

As an analyst, I have to provide information about how the revenue is chaning with time, what are the major contributors to the revenue and what factors causing declining in overall revenue.

### 4.2 Head of Sales

The decision Head of Sales is to make it that what are the products which are selling/in-demand in a particular region. How to increase sales in that particular region.

As an analyst, I have to provide him information about the products which draws most of the revenue and in-demand in a particular region.

### 4.3 Marketing Manager

The decision Marketing Manager is to make it that what are the marketing campaigns which are driving the most traffic and conversions. How to improve the marketing campaigns.

As an analyst, I have to provide him information about the marketing campaigns which are driving the most traffic and conversions and how to improve the marketing campaigns.

### 4.4 Operations Manager

The decision Operations Manager is to make it that which regions are facing delivery delays and reasons behind it. Also to ensure that the products are delivered on time.

As an analyst, I have to provide him information about the regions which are facing delivery delays and reasons behind it and also provide information about the products which are not being delivered on time.

### 4.5 Seller Management Team

The decision Seller Management Team is to make it that which sellers are performing well and which sellers need improvement.

As an analyst, I have to provide him information about the sellers who are performing well and which sellers need improvement and also provide information about the products which are not being delivered on time.

### 4.6 Customer Experience Team

The decision Customer Experience Team is to make it that which customers are genuine and potential customers and how to improve the customer experience.

As an analyst, I have to provide him information about the customers who are genuine and potential customers and also provide information about the products which are not being delivered on time.

## 5. Stakeholder Requirements

## 6. Business Questions
### 6.1 CEO
1. How is ShopSphere's revenue changing over time?
2. Which product categories, products, and regions contribute the most to overall revenue?
3. Which categories, products, or regions are underperforming in terms of revenue?
4. What factors are associated with periods of revenue decline?
5. Which areas of the business should management prioritize for growth?


### 6.2 Head Of Sales
1. Which products and categories generate the highest sales in each region?
2. Which regions have strong demand but relatively low sales performance?
3. Which products are consistently high-performing across multiple regions?
4. Which product categories show opportunities for regional sales growth?
5. How does sales performance vary across sellers, products, categories, and regions?


## 6.3 Marketing Manager
1. Which customer segments generate the highest revenue?
2. Which customer segments have the highest repeat-purchase behavior?
3. Which product categories are most frequently purchased by different customer segments?
4. Which regions have a strong customer base but relatively low purchasing activity?
5. What customer behavior patterns could help guide future marketing strategies?


### 6.4 Operations Manager
1. Which regions experience the highest delivery delays?
2. Which factors are associated with delivery delays?
3. Which sellers have the highest rate of late deliveries?
4. Which regions and sellers consistently deliver orders ahead of or within the expected delivery time?
5. Where should operational improvements be prioritized to reduce delivery delays?

### 6.5 Seller Management Team
1. Which sellers generate the highest revenue and order volume?
2. Which sellers have the best delivery performance?
3. Which sellers have poor performance across revenue, delivery, and customer satisfaction metrics?
4. Which sellers receive consistently low customer ratings?
5. Which sellers require performance improvement, and what areas should they prioritize?

### 6.6 Customer Experience Team
1. Which customers demonstrate high-value and repeat-purchase behavior?
2. Which products, categories, and sellers receive the highest and lowest customer ratings?
3. Is there an association between delivery delays and customer satisfaction?
4. Which regions have the highest concentration of loyal and high-value customers?
5. Which customer or operational factors are associated with poor customer experience?
